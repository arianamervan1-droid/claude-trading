# $20k/month Pipeline

Target: $20,000/month combined from 2 stores + 1 platform (stashly.org). Working split: stores $8-12k, platform $8-12k (to refine once Stashly's model is known).
Fanvue (@that_lily) is treated as a traffic/revenue channel; its chats and account policy are handled by the owner.
This pipeline covers content, offers, analytics, and store/platform ops only.

## Weekly loop
1. **Mon - Report**: pull Shopify (ShopifyQL: sales, sessions, conversion, top products, referrers) and Fanvue earnings summary; compare to weekly goal ($4.6k = 20k/4.33).
2. **Mon - Decide**: double down on top 2 products/content themes; cut bottom performers.
3. **Tue-Wed - Produce**: batch a week of content (short video, photos, captions) per channel.
4. **Thu - Schedule**: queue posts (Metricool) with tracking links per channel (Fanvue tracking links, Shopify UTM links).
5. **Fri - Offers**: one promo per store (discount code) and one Fanvue promotion/free-trial link; email/SMS campaign via Klaviyo.
6. **Daily (10 min)**: check orders, inventory, abandoned carts, new subscribers.

## Store setup checklist (each store)
- [ ] Upgrade off trial plan; payments + shipping configured
- [ ] 10-20 products, 3-5 collections, bundles for AOV $40+
- [ ] Klaviyo flows: welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back
- [ ] Discount codes: first-order, bundle, VIP
- [ ] Analytics: sessions, conversion, AOV tracked weekly

## KPI math
| Stream | Goal | Drivers |
|---|---|---|
| Store A+B | $8-12k | ~250-400 orders x $40 AOV; 15-25k visits at 1.5-2% CVR |
| Stashly (platform) | $8-12k | TBD: depends on Stashly's revenue model (fees, subscriptions, sales) |
| Fanvue (channel) | included above | ~400-600 subs x $15-20 + tips/PPV; traffic from IG/TikTok/X |

## Milestones
- Month 1: stores live, content engine running, first $2-4k
- Month 2: scale winning ads/content, email flows live, $8-12k
- Month 3: $15-20k, optimize margins
